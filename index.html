Checkbox Labs

Checkbox Labs is an AI research lab. We test what large language models actually do, as opposed to what they appear to do, and we publish what we find, including results that cut against our own hypotheses.

How we work

Every study is written down before it runs: the hypotheses, the analysis, and the rules for reading the results. We write and test our own code, run open-weight models on our own hardware, and check model outputs against public records rather than taking a model's word for it.

Current study

Do language models invent sources to please the person asking?

Ask a model for a source and it will sometimes name a paper, study or court case that does not exist. Our current study asks whether models trained to be helpful do this more when the user signals what they want to hear: that they believe a source exists, or that they hope one does.

We compare four open-weight models with their post-trained versions on academic, medical and legal questions, and we look inside each model for an internal signal that a citation is made up. The study plan will be registered before any data are collected.
