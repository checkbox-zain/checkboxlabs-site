Do language models tell you what is true, or what you want to hear? Checkbox Labs is an AI research lab that tests questions like this with pre-registered experiments, open-weight models and public records.

Studies

Are fake citations a form of reward hacking?

Preprint on arXiv, 2026

On 900 questions across twelve open-weight checkpoints, asking for sources raised fabrication by 47.8 points in post-trained models and not in base models. Our registered headline prediction did not hold, and the paper says so.

Do language models invent sources to please the person asking?

In design

We compare four open-weight base models with their post-trained versions on academic, medical and legal questions, vary what the user says they believe or hope, and look inside each model for a signal that a citation is made up. The plan will be registered before any data are collected.

How we work

Hypotheses, analysis and decision rules are fixed before data collection. We write and test our own code, run open-weight models on our own hardware, and check each citation against Crossref, Semantic Scholar, OpenAlex, PubMed and CourtListener.
